# SYNTHIOSIS

**Creation through coexistence. “Synching the thinking.”**
GGI — Genial Genuine Intelligence · *Lumină, nu vrăjeală.* (Light, not trickery.)

**▶ Open the hub:** https://ovidemetrian.github.io/SYNTHIOSIS/

SYNTHIOSIS is a framework for working with AI without handing it your thinking. The human brings intent and conscience, the machine brings compute and scale, and every claim must be verifiable tomorrow. This repository is the hub: one page that gathers the laws, the instruments and the applied work, with a short explanation of each.

## Governance
- **[The Ten Laws of AI](https://10lawsofai.com)**: human-facing and developer's editions.
- **[GGI Code v1.0 & Standard](https://github.com/ovidemetrian/GGI-GENIAL-GENUINE-INTELLIGENCE/tree/main/code)**: the Laws as a prompt any AI reads first, plus a developer standard.
- **[GGI Law Checker](https://ovidemetrian.github.io/GGI-GENIAL-GENUINE-INTELLIGENCE/laws/)**: check an AI reply against the Laws and seal it to a verifiable record.

## Instruments
- **[GGI Control Panel](https://ovidemetrian.github.io/GGI-GENIAL-GENUINE-INTELLIGENCE/)**: one prompt through connectors to many AIs, synthesized under the Meh → But → Aha → Pám Pám sequence.
- **[GGI Light Panel](https://ovidemetrian.github.io/GGI-GENIAL-GENUINE-INTELLIGENCE/panel/)**: the quick way to put the GGI Code into any AI from a phone.
- **[Möbius Bar Desk](https://ovidemetrian.github.io/mobiusbar/)**: three agents that check each other on news and claims.

## Applied
- **[Director's Desk](https://ovidemetrian.github.io/directors-desk/)**: a broadcast desk where the human directs and the machine operates.
- **[InstaJam](https://ovidemetrian.github.io/INSTAJAM/)**: hum it, write it, make it, mix it, ship it.
- **[SONOS Panel (concept)](https://ovidemetrian.github.io/SONOS-panel/)**: a room-first whole-home audio controller. Unofficial, with simulated controls.
- **[SONOR & ACTION](https://ovidemetrian.github.io/ACTION/)**: songs and videos made into gifts someone keeps.
